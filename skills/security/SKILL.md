## Immediate priorities

1. Protect people and contain the affected account or endpoint without destroying evidence.
2. Record time, affected assets, observed indicators and actions already taken.
3. Preserve emails, alerts and logs; do not delete suspicious files merely to make symptoms disappear.
4. Escalate immediately to the security response team.

Do not ask the user to send secrets or malware samples through chat. Do not promise that the incident is contained.
