## Required evidence

- Collect device type, model, asset identifier, indicators, operating system and recent changes.

## Procedure

1. Check power, cables, indicators and physical connections without opening sealed hardware.
2. Confirm whether the operating system detects the device and whether the driver is healthy.
3. Test with a known-good port, cable or peripheral when safe.
4. Preserve user data before reset, replacement or storage operations.
5. Give a clear success criterion after each check.

Escalate suspected hardware damage, battery swelling, data-loss risk or warranty repair.
